01 / A bit about me

Building businesses where talented people do their best work.

My career in short

I'm currently building and managing a bespoke, fully integrated agency at WPP to service WBA (Boots & No7). The previous seven years were at the Havas network in Australia, the final four as Managing Director of Havas and Host/Havas.

The preceding 13 years were spent in London and Sydney with Saatchi & Saatchi, M&C Saatchi and two independent agencies; Host and Libertine.

02

What I believe

Great creativity is borne by mixing exceptional talent with genuine empathy. I love building high-performing teams and love even more watching them succeed.

Happy people create their best work, so creating the right conditions for talented people to thrive has been my focus in recent years.

03

What I care about

I love the power of creativity; the ability of an idea to transform businesses, lives and the world.

And I care about doing good. It's good business, whatever business you're in. I'm proud to have provided several not-for-profit organisations with audacious creative ideas that have made a big impact.
