HAVAS // DEFENCE FORCE RECRUITING (DFR)

Building a long-term recruitment platform.

I ran the DFR account from 2014–2016 before becoming MD of Havas, then ran this flagship client at an executive level. The work won the Australian Grand Effie (Effectiveness Grand Prix) in 2018 and a Gold Effie for Long-term Effects covering 2014–2018.

The wider DFR programme included brand campaigns for the Navy, Army and Air Force; a rebuilt, re-platformed and reimagined DefenceJobs.gov.au digital experience; a national VR recruitment programme; the ADF Active app; and 'See Yourself' Indigenous recruitment content.
